# Visual check of the gallery screenshots

Phase 5, run by the lead at commit `170b09c` after `pnpm --filter @zqhoot/web test:e2e` (756 tests passed) regenerated every screenshot. The gallery covers 57 screens in 10 Playwright projects: the six breakpoints of ADR-0016 (320, 390, 768, 1366, 1920 and 3840 wide) plus dark, forced colours, more contrast and reduced motion at 390, and three dedicated 400-player lobby captures.

Nine reviewers each opened one group of screens at a set of projects and reported visual defects. A second reviewer per group re-opened the cited images, confirmed or rejected each finding, and located the cause in the source. Reduced motion was not inspected separately, because its captures match `phone-390` apart from animation, which a still image cannot show.

## Result

95 findings were reported: 70 confirmed (2 high, 14 medium, 54 low) and 25 rejected. Several confirmed findings are the same defect seen at different projects. They were routed to three fix tasks, each with the lead's decision per finding:

- [V1-presenter-visual](../tasks/V1-presenter-visual.md): presenter stage.
- [V2-player-visual](../tasks/V2-player-visual.md): player, join and landing screens, and the shared button styles.
- [V3-host-editor-visual](../tasks/V3-host-editor-visual.md): host, editor and gallery fixtures.

The specs quote every confirmed finding in full. VC-48 (the roster's inner scroll area) was declined, with the reason in the V3 spec.
